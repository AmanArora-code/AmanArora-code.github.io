I'm an Application Developer at Orgzit and an MCA graduate from Galgotias University, based in Nirman Vihar, Delhi and open to remote work.

At the same time, I genuinely enjoy sitting with business owners and clients, understanding their problems and shaping the solution. I'm comfortable on both sides, client-facing solutions and hands-on development, and flexible about where I add the most value.
